# Contributing to Apple Core

Thank you for considering a contribution. A few licensing facts constrain what the project can accept.

## The two tracks

Apple Core's **source** at `github.com/oliverames/apple-core` is available under the GNU General Public License version 3 or any later version (GPL-3.0-or-later). The **official signed binary** Oliver distributes via Gumroad is conveyed under `EULA.md` with license-key activation (see `docs/licensing.md`). The binary contains no GPL-licensed third-party code, so a keyed binary can legally be the EULA-distribution channel while the source continues to offer GPL build freedom.

By submitting, you agree to both halves of that split:

## Grant you make

By submitting a patch, pull request, or other contribution to Apple Core, you agree that your contribution is licensed under GPL-3.0-or-later as published by the Free Software Foundation, **and** you grant Oliver Ames a perpetual, worldwide, non-exclusive, royalty-free right to relicense, sublicense, and distribute your contribution as part of Apple Core's official binary distribution under any license Oliver chooses, including the commercial EULA.

This dual grant is the only mechanism that lets the source stay GPL while the signed binaries you buy can remain keyed. You retain copyright in your contribution; you are granting Oliver an additional right, not transferring ownership.

## What this rules out

Do not submit code copied from GPL-3.0-only third-party projects unless you hold a separate commercial relicense, and never submit code whose copyright you do not own without the copyright holder's permission, because the second grant above would be impossible.

## Code of conduct

This contribution policy is the project's entire code of conduct for outside patches. Be direct, be accurate, and cite your sources. See also `LICENSE.md` (the GPL text), `THIRD_PARTY_LICENSES/`, and `NOTICE` for donor provenance.
